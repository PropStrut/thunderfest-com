"Fearless" Fred Alter's Scrapbook Photos

We're excited to present this collection of photos loaned to us by "Fearless" Fred Alter. He is one of many distinguished powerboat racers from the Detroit area to be inducted into the Unlimited Hydroplane Hall of Fame.

All 78 pics are shown as thumbnail photos below, in order of general appeal. Click on any thumbnail to see the larger JPG version (they average 35K-65K bytes each).
