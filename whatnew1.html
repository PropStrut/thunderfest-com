March 17, 1998

NOTED MARINE ARTIST JIM CLARY SELECTED
TO CREATE THUNDERFEST COLLECTOR PRINTS

The Spirit of Detroit Thunderfest, WDIV TV-4 and world renowned marine
artist Jim Clary are teaming up for the first time ever! Jim Clary will be
creating a series of four collector prints celebrating a Century of
Hydroplane Racing on the Detroit River.

The 1998 print will be unveiled June 25th - until then you'll have to
wait with all of us for an image of it. Click here for more details.
