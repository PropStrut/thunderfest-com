Quake on the Lake - Results 2002

These results & records are not official until approved by the APBA!!

The third annual Quake on the Lake III on Pontiac Lake provided action packed racing along with new APBA World Records set on a one mile course in the APBA Hydroplane Super Series July 27 & 28, 2002. Quake on the Lake is developing into one of the hot stops on the tour with the beautiful race site setting along with world record water conditions on the one mile track.

New APBA records were set in the 1.5 Litre Stocks and 1 Litre Modified classes for a five mile heat on a one mile course. First, the 1.5 Stock heat race on Saturday saw Mark Johnson set a new record in James Dixon's T-23 Wanna Bee at 63.420 MPH. Johnson's mark fell hours later when George Kennedy Jr. in Bob Wilson's T-80 Outlaw went 64.018 MPH in the 1.5 Stock championship heat. On Sunday in the heat race Jimmy Shane in the T-2000 Millenium Eagle broke the record again at 64.235 MPH. Tom Thompson driving Joe Cheezum's Y-26 Miss Zandy broke the 1 Litre Modified record three times over the weekend. On Saturday in the heat race Thompson clocked in at 72.304 MPH. In the final heat on Saturday Thompson broke the record again at 72.748 MPH. Thompson and Cheezum pushed the mark up to 73.117 MPH in the heat race on Sunday.

Jeff Ayler - Madison, Indiana [Ed: Thanks for the report, Jeff!]
