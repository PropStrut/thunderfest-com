Past Winners of the Gold Cup

The Gold Cup has long been one of the most prestigious trophies in all of motorsports. Parker Jones has kindly provided a list of all the past Gold Cup winners, dating back to 1904.

Look for Chip Hanauer's 11 wins, and Bernie Little's 14 as an owner.
We also have a page that describes the Gold Cup Racing Format Over The Years.
