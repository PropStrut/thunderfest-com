These are more generally related sites:
  • Detroit River Festival. Antique boats, artists, memorabilia, exhibits, demos, and more.
  • Hydroplane & Raceboat Museum. Located in Seattle. Vintage Gold Cup Racers!
  • Dossin Great Lakes Museum. Detroit's own jewel with the U-99 Miss Pepsi on exhibit.
  • Antique and Race Boat Society. Preservation and enjoyment of historic, antique and classic boats.
  • Motorsports Hall Of Fame in Novi, MI. Don't miss the page about Miss U.S., the boat that held the mile prop-powered speed record for 38 years! The 200mph+ record by Roy Duby [1962] was broken in June 2000 by Russ Wicks in the Miss Freei hydroplane, at 205.494 MPH. The photo on this page is as Miss U.S. is displayed at the Motorsports Hall of Fame in Novi, MI.
  • Home Page of the Grand Prix Hydroplanes. About the boats and teams.
  • Ken Warby has held the absolute water speed record for 20 years now (jet powered @ 317.6 mph), and he is building a new boat! More pics.
  • Mariner's Museum. Many features, including a collection of Chris Craft boats and documentation.
  • Mystic Seaport. The Museum of America and the Sea. Historic Tall Ships.
  • Great Lakes Cruiser magazine Online. Dedicated to all Great Lakes boaters.
  • BoatRacing.com. Racing info about all powerboat classes.
  • Powerboating. As covered by the Mining Company.
  • The Waterline. A Vancouver based page for power and sail boaters.

  • Michigan Ice Racing Association. Auto racing on local lakes. (Winter-time of course!)

  • Roostertail Club. The posh restaurant next to the Dodge Pits in Detroit.
  • Race Rock. The motorsport fan's dream restaurant!

  • Detroit Red Wings. 1997 Stanley cup Champions!
