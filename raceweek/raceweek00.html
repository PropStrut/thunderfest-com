The Chrysler Jeep Detroit APBA Gold Cup
Raceweek Schedule for July 6-9, 2000

THIS IS LAST YEARS SCHEDULE. IT DOESN'T CHANGE MUCH YEAR-TO-YEAR SO WE'VE LEFT IT POSTED FYI. UPDATES WILL BE MADE IN JUNE.

Thursday and Friday are practice and qualifying days, and there is an Automotive Gold Cup race Friday afternoon. Saturday is a full day of racing and Sunday is the BIG day .. the final day of racing. Below, you'll find lists of raceweek highlights and parties around town, along with a detailed schedule of the action.

WWJ Radio will be broadcasting LIVE from the event all weekend, covering each heat of Unlimited Hydroplane racing. Bring your radio with you!

Raceweek Highlights:
  • FRIDAY: Detailed radio-control models of Hydroplanes past & present will be on display in Waterworks Park. Look near the trophy presentation stand and small white Unlimiteds Detroit merchandise trailer.

  • FRIDAY 5:15pm: The Chrysler Jeep Automotive Gold Cup race is a one-heat winner-take-all event for the Unlimited Lights.

  • FRIDAY 6:30pm: The HYDROFEST Benefit party. At the beautiful Detroit Yacht Club, this event is open to the public (you need not be a DYC or SODT member). Show your support for Thunderfest and the Wertz Warriors of the Special Olympics, Michigan. Festivities include auctions and special presentations along with dinner by the DYC. Individual tickets are $150 each and we anticipate a sellout. Casual, nautical attire. Call us at (800) 359-7760 for more info.

  • SATURDAY 4:30: The Unlimited Drivers Autograph Session - in front of the Big Boy Cafe in Waterworks Park. Free! Donations are accepted and all proceeds go to the Drivers Relief Fund. Arrive early!

  • SUNDAY: FINAL heats for all classes.

  • SATURDAY AND SUNDAY: Julie Clark's "Mopar T-34" Julie is an award-winning veteran pilot and North America's only solo T-34 aerobatics performer. Watch for her exciting action above the river (tentative times listed below).

Raceweek Parties Around Town:

After the roar and thunder of the boats have calmed for the day, check out these local bars and restaurants and get ready to start your engines up for more thrills and chills.

  • Thursday June 29 - MR. Bs / Royal Oak
    215 South Main Street, Royal Oak 248-399-0017
    The Thunderfest and the Home of Rock n Roll, WRIF, will be invading the heart of fashionable Royal Oak. Have some food and drinks, shoot some pool, and collect WRIF & Thunderfest giveaways. Call Ted at the Thunderfest for more details or directions at 313-331-7770

  • Monday July 3 - Beach Grill
    24420 Jefferson, St. Clair Shores 810-771-4455
    Kick off raceweek 2000 at the East Sides true America bistro located on beautiful Lake St. Clair. Come party with the Thunderfest on the Beach Grills 4500 sq. ft deck or pull right up to their waterfront dockage. Race teams will be getting in to town on this date and will be invited to come rub elbows with you, the fans.

  • Wednesday July 5 - Wild Woodys
    32500 Gratiot, Roseville 810-294-5331
    Come out & help us rock the hottest bar on Gratiot with Thunderfest and 101.1 FM WRIF. The DOC Doug Podell will be on hand to host what has become a metro-Detroit tradition on Wednesday nights. Last year, several race teams joined the Thunderfest party and will hopefully be in attendance this year. Now that we have teamed up with rock giant WRIF this night will qualify as a must attend event!

  • Thursday July 6 - Jacks Waterfront Restaurant
    24214 Jefferson Ave, St. Clair shores 810-445-8080
    Head east down Jefferson Avenue to the nautical mile area. Located on Lake St. Clair this favorite East Side hot spot features delicious dining and lots of fun. Join Thunderfest & Look for drivers & hydros to be featured at this pre-race party.

  • Friday July 7 - Hydrofest 2000
    At the Detroit Yacht Club
    Join us at the DYC for dinner, dancing, and both a silent & live auction. Tickets must be purchased in advance proceeds will benefit Special Olympics, Michigan and Spirit of Detroit Thunderfest. For more info & tickets call Krista 313-331-7770

Tune to WRIF 101.1 FM & Watch for info in the next Signal Flag for June party dates & locations (Currently scheduled for June 16, 20, 22, and 30)
