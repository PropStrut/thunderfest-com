The Chrysler Jeep Superstores Detroit APBA Gold Cup
Raceweek Schedule for July 11-14, 2002

Thursday and Friday are practice and qualifying days, and there is an Automotive Gold Cup race Friday afternoon. Saturday is a full day of racing and Sunday is the BIG day .. the final day of racing. Below, you'll find lists of raceweek highlights and parties around town, along with a detailed schedule of the action.

Raceweek Highlights

  • WEDNESDAY July 10 6:30pm: The HYDROFEST Fundraiser party.
    NOTE NEW DATE! At the beautiful Roostertail Restaurant, this event is open to the public. Show your support for Thunderfest! Festivities include auctions and special presentations along with food and open bar by the Roostertail. Tickets are $50 each. Casual, nautical attire. Call us at (800) 359-7760 for more info. Early-bird reservations include choice of free Thursday or Friday pit pass!

  • FRIDAY 5:15pm: The Chrysler Jeep Automotive Gold Cup race is an exciting one-heat winner-take-all event for the automotive-powered Unlimited Lights.

  • SATURDAY 4:30 (approx): The Unlimited Drivers Autograph Session.
    Free! Waterworks Park. Donations are accepted and all proceeds go to the Drivers Relief Fund. Listen to the PA for exact time and location (subject to change). Don't be late!

  • SATURDAY AND SUNDAY: Julie Clark's "Mopar Part" T-34. Julie is an award-winning veteran pilot and North America's only solo T-34 aerobatics performer. Watch for her exciting action above the river (tentative times listed below).

  • SUNDAY: FINAL heats for all classes.

Parties Around Town:

After the roar and thunder of the boats have calmed for the day, check out these local bars and restaurants and get ready to start your engines up for more thrills and chills.
