The Chrysler Jeep Detroit APBA Gold Cup
Raceweek Schedule for July 12-15, 2001

Thursday and Friday are practice and qualifying days, and there is an Automotive Gold Cup race Friday afternoon. Saturday is a full day of racing and Sunday is the BIG day .. the final day of racing. Below, you'll find lists of raceweek highlights and parties around town, along with a detailed schedule of the action.

Raceweek Highlights

  • THURSDAY 6:00pm: The Unlimited Hydroplane Hall Of Fame induction ceremony. Join us in honoring the 2001 Inductees and their contributions to hydroplane history. Hosted by "Dossin's", at the Roostertail Restaurant (next to the Dodge Pits at the foot of Marquette). Several Detroiters will be inducted! Doors open at 6pm. Tickets are $25, for all the details visit http://www.unlimitedsdetroit.com/uhom.

  • FRIDAY 5:15pm: The Chrysler Jeep Automotive Gold Cup race is an exciting one-heat winner-take-all event for the automotive-powered Unlimited Lights.

  • FRIDAY 6:00pm: The HYDROFEST Benefit party. At the beautiful Detroit Yacht Club, this event is open to the public (you need not be a DYC or SODT member). Show your support for Thunderfest and the Wertz Warriors of the Special Olympics, Michigan. Festivities include auctions and special presentations along with dinner by the DYC. Individual tickets are $150 each and we anticipate a sellout. Casual, nautical attire. Call us at (800) 359-7760 for more info.

  • SATURDAY 4:30 or 5:00: The Unlimited Drivers Autograph Session.
    Presented by BankOne. Free! Waterworks Park. Donations are accepted and all proceeds go to the Drivers Relief Fund. Listen to the PA for exact time and location (subject to change). Don't be late!

  • SATURDAY AND SUNDAY: Julie Clark's "Mopar T-34" Julie is an award-winning veteran pilot and North America's only solo T-34 aerobatics performer. Watch for her exciting action above the river (tentative times listed below).

  • SUNDAY: FINAL heats for all classes.
