*** THE AUTOMOTIVE GOLD CUP WAS CANCELLED FOR FRIDAY

Without any official notification, the Unlimited Lights testing
and qualifying as well as the AGC start times came and went.
Not one UL has hit the water.  It seems politics plays the role,
with most or all of it out of the control of Thunderfest.
Everyone at SODT and our fans are hoping an agreement can be
reached to run the weekends UL races ... the event takes a big
hit without them.


*** FRIDAY RACING BY THE OUTBOARD PERFORMANCE INFLATABLES

A complete timing of the only racing held on Friday has been
posted, direct from the crew at Thunderfest Timing and Scoring:
  http://thunderfest.com/news/02/2002GoldCupResultsFriday.pdf
I missed it, but I understand there was an incident in one of
the heats with 2 or 3 boats coming together and 4 racers in the
water.  At least one boat was damaged - looks like the righthand
tube was cut open by a prop at the rear.  I don't believe there
were any injuries but it was a close call.
