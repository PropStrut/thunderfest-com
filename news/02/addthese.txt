I can't remember all the stories so chime in ...

What I remember most was his explanation that he was all about the -racing-.  When asked about his interest in vintage hydros, he said it wasn't his bag... that he was never
much interested in the boats themselves (though he had a couple favs and beasts).
If I have my hulls right, I think he said the one he'd like to take for a ride now is the Bud that just came back from Australia, with it's Rolls-Royce powerplant.

Overheard at the signing table, he said he didn't keep any of the trophies.  The question
was furthered by 'but you get the little trophy to keep when it's a permanent trophy' (eg:
Gold Cup) ... 'yea, well, afterwards, kids would be looking at it with such awe that I would
give it to one of them' ... 'half the time it didn't make it as far as my car'.

Chip also spoke about Jim Kropfeld as a driver he greatly admired.  After mentioning the great driving he'd seen Jim do in their early days, he delved further.  'We would do nasty, nasty things to each other on the course, but after the race it was all good'.  "He did make me buy lots of new underwear".  

Chip seemed to wish he'd lost his second Gold Cup to Jim, in '83 (the second of seven straight) ... 'Jim's motor let go with half a lap left.  As a driver, he won that race.'
'At the time we expected that he would come back to win the Gold Cup another year, but

[cancer]

now we know that didn't happen'.
