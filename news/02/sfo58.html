*** THE AUTOMOTIVE GOLD CUP WAS CANCELLED FOR FRIDAY

Without any official notification, the Unlimited Lights testing and qualifying as well as the AGC start times came and went. Not one UL has hit the water. It seems politics plays the role, with most or all of it out of the control of Thunderfest. Everyone at SODT and our fans are hoping an agreement can be reached to run the weekends UL races ... the event takes a big hit without them.
