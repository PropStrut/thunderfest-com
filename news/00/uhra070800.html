3:31 PM update...

Here are your results from Unlimited Heat 2B...
At the start the hulls lined up with the U-5 Appian Jeronimo on the inside, the U-6 Miss Madison in lane two, the U-25 Miss Freei in lane three, the U-8 Llumar Window Film in lane four and the U-2 Miss Chrysler Jeep in lane five.  It was a good start with the U-5 Appian Jeronimo grabbing the early lead.  George Stratton held that lead and did not give it up this time out.

1st ... U-5 Appian Jeronimo
2nd ... U-2 Miss Chrysler Jeep
3rd ... U-6 Miss Madison
4th ... U-8 Llumar Window Film
5th ... U-25 Miss Freei

3:11 PM update...

Here are your results from Unlimited Heat 2A...
As they lined up for the start, U-1 Miss Budweiser had lane one, U-10 York and Shelby Enterprises presents Miss DYC had lane two, U-9 Carpenters Communications had lane three, U-16 Miss E-Lam Plus in four and U-3 Vacationville.com completed the field for in lane five.

The start for this one was good and impressive.  As they came out of the first turn, the U-1, U-10, U-9 and U-16 were side by side.  By the end of the second turn they had separated into two packs.  The U-1 and U-10 up front and the U-9 and U-16 just off the lead.

After lap one the field was set with U-1 in the lead, U-10 in second, U-9 in third, U-16 in forth and U-3 in fifth.

In the end...
1st ... U-1 Miss Budweiser
2nd ... U-10 York and Shelby Enterprises presents Miss DYC
3rd ... U-9 Carpenter Communications
4th ... U-16 Miss E-Lam Plus
5th ... U-3 Vacationville.com

1:32 PM update...
Here are your results from Unlimited Heat 1B...
We had to wait for the official word on this one.  As the heat ended we had word that everyone except for the U-16 Miss E-Lam Plus jumped the gun.  There was also word that the U-2 Miss Chrysler Jeep had crowded the the U-16 and was assessed a one lap penalty.  The one lap penalty on the U-2 stood, but the field had not jumped.

In any case, the U-2 Miss Chrysler Jeep and the U-10 York and Shelby Enterprises presents Miss DYC put on an amazing show for the fans.  They were running together right through the the finish of lap one.  The U-6 Miss Madison was back just a bit off the lead with the U-9 Carpenters Communications following the U-6.  The U-16 Miss E-Lam Plus trailed the field.  At the end of lap two, the U-2 pulled ahead of the U-10.

After full review of the tapes, the U-9 turned out to be the only boat to jump the gun.

In the end...
1st ... U-10 York and Shelby Enterprises presents Miss DYC
2nd ... U-6 Miss Madison
3rd ... U-16 Miss E-Lam Plus
4th ... U-2 Miss Chrysler Jeep
5th ... U-9 Carpenters Communications

1:05 PM update...
Here are your results from Unlimited Heat 1A...
The heat was a solid race for first for much of the first lap.  The U-1 Miss Budweiser and the U-5 Appian Jeronimo ran a close race coming through the second turn.  As the U-1 began to pull ahead of the U-5 a new battle developed for second place as the U-8 Llumar Window Film advanced to run with the U-5.  The battle for second remained within striking distance of the lead.  As the fans cheered, it was announced that the U-8 was actually one lap down due to going inside a buoy during lap 1.  As the U-8 and the U-5 continued to race, the U-1 started pulling away from the pack.  The U-3 Vacationville.com and the U-25 Miss Freei trailed the leaders.  Late in the race, the U-8 spun in Roostertail turn.  Jimmy King attempted to refire the hull, kicked out a lot of smoke and sat dead in the water.  The U-3 veered out to miss the U-8 as it sat (roughly lane three) then the U-8 was able to restart.

Unofficial order of finish...
1st ... U-1 Miss Budweiser
2nd ... U-5 Appian Jeronimo
3rd ... U-3 Vacationville.com
4th ... U-25 Miss Freei
5th ... U-8 Llumar Window Film

12:27 PM update...
Here are your results from Unlimited Light Heat 1B...
The Automotive Gold Cup winning UL-72 Budweiser/Pocket Mechanic started slow and looked to be well positioned for the launch of the race, but the boat was beat to the gun as the race got underway.  The UL-110 Miss CompAir went down prior to the finish.  In the end both the UL-102 and the UL-72 also went down but they did managed to finish.  Both boats appeared to lose power during the heat.  It appears several hulls on the Belle Isle side are getting weeds in the intake and overheating during the race. 

Final order of finish...
1st ... GP-7 Casino de Montreal
2nd ... GP-59  Baby Doll
3rd ... UL-72  Budweiser/Pocket Mechanic
4th ... UL-102  McGregor/Edward E. Jones
5th ... UL-141 Authorized Cellular
DNS ... UL-110  Miss CompAir
