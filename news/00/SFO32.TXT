THE CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT *APBA GOLD CUP*         July 6-9, 2000


Hello from everyone at SODT in our first SFO issue of the New
Year.  This warm weekend in Detroit is our first hint of summer,
and it almost feels like it's time to setup the tents!  Related
events are already upon us:


*** THUNDERFEST VOLUNTEER SOCIAL --- THIS TUESDAY!  (FEB 29TH)

We had a great turnout last year, so we're doing it again...
Join the Officers and Staff of Thunderfest for a casual
gathering with fun, food, beverages, door prizes, a 50/50
raffle, and all the latest Thunderfest and hydro racing info.

Free for volunteers, guests $10.  6pm - 9pm, at the Bruce Post
VFW Hall on Jefferson (about 11 1/2 mile).  This is not a
mandatory meeting.

As of January 1st, dues were increased to $40.  However, if you 
pay your dues at the party you will get the early-bird rate of $30.


*** COMMODORE / VOLUNTEER BALL 2000 --- SATURDAY MARCH 11TH

Commodore J. Brian Elliot and The Flag Officers present a night 
of Dinner, Dancing and Special Prizes at the beautiful Detroit 
Yacht Club.  Cruise into the new millenium in recognition of all
Thunderfest volunteers, friends of Thunderfest, and to welcome 
in the new Flag Officers.

$30 per person - $55 per couple, all reservations must be paid in
full by March 6th - seating is limited.  CASUAL BOATING ATTIRE.
Cash bar with complimentary beer, wine and soft drinks.  Call 
the SODT offices for more info: (800) 359-7760 or (313) 331-7770.


*** "UNLIMITEDS DETROIT" MEMBERS PREVIEW THE "MADISON" MOVIE

Unlimiteds Detroit is not directly associated with SODT, but many
people belong to both groups.  At the recent UD meeting they 
showed clips from the upcoming movie based around the 1971 Gold
Cup win by Jim McCormick in Miss Madison... it's been a long
time since a movie has included Hydroplane racing, and this
looks like it will be good!  Local legend Fred Alter was on
hand to talk about the making of the hydroplane scenes.


*** "UNLIMITEDS DETROIT" MOVIE DAY --- SATURDAY MARCH 25TH

"The Revenge of UD at the Movies" at the Dossin Museum on Belle
Isle.  11am Swap Meet with movies at Noon featuring Gar Wood
Jr. on his dad plus the 1999 Hawaii race (watch Miss Bud in the
3rd heat - you be the judge).  Members and guests invited (bring
a friend and your swapping stuff).  A donation to the museum is
suggested at the door... if you've never been to the Dossin,
it's a great place to visit.  The Dossin phone number is
(313) 852-4051.  For a map, see location 5 on this page:
  http://www.thunderfest.com/spectate/bimap2.html


*** VILLWOCK INJURED BY R/C PLANE

During a test firing of his radio-control plane, the restraint
failed and Villwock was injured.  The 10 foot model is powered
by a 10 horsepower engine with a 26 inch prop --- interestingly,
much larger than Miss Bud's own prop.  He suffered a broken bone
in his forearm and damaged his middle fingertip.  He spent the
night - his birthday - in the hospital and will wear his arm in
a brace for the next six weeks.

"It's a little like trying to stop your lawn mower with your 
bare hands, but I've been through a lot worse," said Villwock,
the UHRA's two-time defending driving champion.  He expects to
be ready for the Miss Budweiser's first preseason test session
on Lake Washington in April.
