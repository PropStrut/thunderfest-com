THE CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT *APBA GOLD CUP*         July 6-9, 2000


*** WEBCAST DETAILS

Sorry about the trouble this afternoon with the broadcast.  The
morning sessions ran well, but the afternoon session didn't.
Links to all the sessions can be found at
  http://www.thunderfest.com/raceweek/coverage.html
The sessions that have already run are archived for your
enjoyment at any time.

On the "Powerboat" email list I made the 'clear your browser
cache' suggestion before knowing the webcast was broken at the
server end.  The problem exhibited itself as stalling on
"Loading..." then mentioning net congestion and "trying for 60
seconds" which is the symptom everyone reported.  A set of
servers that were rebooted failed to come back up properly.  It
was not a bandwidth issue, and we don't think there will be.

Tomorrow's show will use cameras located in Pit Corner, on
top of Pit Tower, and at the far end of the bleachers.  I
believe that tomorrows live coverage will be much more
successful and enjoyable - see you there!
