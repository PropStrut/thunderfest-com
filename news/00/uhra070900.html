A look back on Sunday's full coverage

7:57 PM update...

The U-16 Miss E-Lam plus lost a gearbox when it went down the second time.

7:15 PM update...

Chrysler-Jeep APBA Gold Cup
The sun came out as the boats took to the water for the Gold Cup.  The U-10 York & Shelby Enterprises presents Miss DYC lined up in lane one, the U-16 Miss E-Lam Plus was in lane two, the U-1 Miss Budweiser was in lane three, the U-5 Appian Jeronimo was in lane four and the U-6 Miss Madison was in lane five.  The U-2 Miss Chrysler Jeep served as the trailer. 

It was a good start as the U-1 Miss Budweiser powered forward with the U-10 York & Shelby Enterprises presents Miss DYC following in close second.  The U-5 Appian Jeronimo was just off the lead two.  The U-2 Miss Chrysler Jeep dueled with the U-6 Miss Madison for fourth.

The U-16 Miss E-Lam Plus went down prior to the start but was able to refire and run for a time.  They went down a second time near the straightaway grandstand and did not make it back into the race.

On lap two the field began to line up with the U-1 in the lead, the U-10 back about two roostertails back.  The U-5 was back from the U-10 about one roostertail.  The U-2 and the U-6 followed the lead pack.  One moment of excitement involved the U-16.  As Mark Evans sat in the U-16 the U-5 powered forward and had to make a right/left decision in a very short amount of time.  George Stratton opted to take the U-5 on the inside and threaded the needle between the U-16 and the wall.  It was so close it brought fans running down out of the stands to see if he made it. 

From there the lead order didn't change.  The U-2 and the U-5 were close and threatened a lead change in the fourth lap, but in the end it was the U-1 Miss Budweiser piloted by Dave Villwock that took the win.

The U-1 finished roughly three roostertails ahead of the U-10.  The U-10 was well ahead of the U-5, but the U-2 was less than a roostertail off second.  The U-6 finished back from the field.

Your order of finish for the Chrysler-Jeep APBA Gold Cup...
1st ... U-1 Miss Budwieser driven by Dave Villwock
2nd ... U-10 York & Shelby Enterprises presents Miss DYC driven by Mark Weber
3rd ... U-5 Appian Jeronimo driven by George Stratton
4th ... U-2 Miss Chrysler-Jeep driven by Mark Tate
5th ... U-6 Miss Madison driven by Jerry Hopp
DNF ... U-16 Miss E-Lam Plus driven by Mark Evans

3:30 PM update...

Unlimited Heat 4A results...
One of the best races I have seen in a while.  It was the U-16 Miss E-Lam Plus and the U-5 Appian Jeronimo putting on an amazing race.  The Mark Weber in the U-16 and George Stratton in theU-5 changed leads with each turn and were side-by-side for three laps.  Both the U-5 and the U-16 were jumping/bouncing out of the water in this battle for the lead.  What a sight!

Not far off the battle for the lead was the U-9 Carpenter Communications.  Mike Hanson never challenged the front two, but was right there with the lead pack.

Just prior to the start, the U-6 Miss Madison was almost washed down in the wake of the leaders.  Jerry Hopp was able to keep the U-6 under power and race to the start.  Jerry trailed the pack until the final lap.  At that time he charged forward and overtook the U-25 Miss Freei to take 4th place.

2:50 PM update...

The Unlimited Light heat ran so close to Unlimited 3B that I remained in the pits to cover both at once.  The UL-10 did flip during the heat, but I want to immediately explain that Charley Wiggins is fine.  The UL-10 Earl's Performance Products was running in second place behind the UL-72 Budweiser/Pocket Mechanic charging toward the lead going into the first turn when the UL-10 cart wheeled, did a full 360 and landed right side up.  Charley's brother-in-law Mitch Moore immediately heard from him over the radio and Charley was up and out of the boat just moments later.  He is currently in the middle of the river on a rescue boat sitting up talking with medical personnel.  Let me again stress that Charley is fine.  I will return with the full results momentarily.

11:31 AM update...

The race committee is on the move to see if they can't get the Playboy/Cigarette boat out onto the water.  

The Playboy/Cigarette Racing Team has joined the HYDRO-PROP as a series sponsor for the 2000 Budweiser Hydroplane Racing Series.  Playboy Magazine and Cigarette Offshore racing boats have partnered in a joint venture to design and build the Playboy/Cigarette Racing Boat.

Cigarette, the premier manufacturer of speedboats, has built this beautiful 35 foot offshore style-racing boat.  Twin 500 EFI Mercury engines power this impressive machine.  An all-female race team always runs the Playboy/Cigarette boat; today's team Captain is Miss Gabrielle Gatti.

When these beautiful ladies are not out on the water, they can be found at the Playboy/Cigarette Racing team merchandise trailer in the HydroMart.  You can purchase Playboy and Cigarette merchandise as well as get autographs and photographs from the girls.

11:18 AM update...

In last nights photo gallery, we added a photo of the IMAX film crew that's shooting here this weekend.  The crew is focusing exclusively on the U-10 York & Shelby Enterprises presents Miss DYC.  The camera being used is one of only 20 in the world and every camera is handmade.  The film will debut in June at the Ford Museum here in Detroit and will be shown on a screen that stands six stories high!  For the U-10 portion of the movie, the film will feature a great corner shot of the U-10 powering through a turn.  There are also a few U-10 dock shots, one shot featuring the Roostertail Restaurant in the background and an engine shot with a small child pointing to the U-10.

10:29 AM update...

Okay, we just had a quick driver's meeting to take a look at the overall situation.  Unlimited heats 3A and 3B have been shortened to three laps.  The final will remain five laps.  We will evaluate heats 4A and 4B when the time comes.  Heats 3A and 3B are currently still set for 11:40 AM and 12:00 PM, but a final decision will be made at 11:10 AM.  Chief Referee Rick Sandstrum explained that there is a general consensus that "we will not going out in this water".

The wind has picked up since the Unlimiteds first took to the water this morning.  It is coming from the west which is the worst possible direction.  The race committee has been keeping the action going with some 5-Liters and vintage boats running a few laps.

During the meeting Mark Weber (driver of the U-10) asked the question on everyone's mind, "what do we need to do to please the fans?"  All the drivers were in agreement that if the heat didn't run they wanted to do something with the fans here in the stands.  In the end a backup plan was set for an addition autograph session at 11:15 am if 3A and 3B are delayed.  Dave Villwock (driver of the U-1) added "let's take the show to the fans if we can't get on the water."

The afternoon webcast has been combined into one show as well.  The morning show will end at 12:30 PM, resume at 12:45 PM and end at 5:00 PM.  This will work to your advantage as there will be less breaks in the broadcast.  When we have no action on the water, the webcast will rebroadcast prior heats, show interviews, etc.

As for the Unlimited Lights, they have been told to remain within fifteen minutes of "ready to run".  They may be forced to only run a final today.
