A look back on Friday's full coverage

11:53 update...

Photos!
On our gallery page, we've just loaded fifteen photos offering a full look back on today's action.

We have a bit of additional information to share on the Automotive Gold Cup...
As we reported earlier, the UL-72 Budweiser/Pocket Mechanic took the win earlier today, but there are a few other stories to tell from the race.

The UL-72 started back from the pack and actually trailed the UL-141 for the first two laps of the race.  As the UL-72 worked to catch Greg Grenier in the UL-141 Authorized Cellular, the UL-141 made a hard left turn and powered back into the turn it had just exited.  What happened?  The hull missed a buoy and had to go back and catch it to avoid penalty.  Be the time the boat made it back into the race, Greg found himself trailing the UL-15 and taking home a third at the end.

The UL-102 was out on the course, but was off the pace and didn't make it to the start/finish time in the required 30 seconds.  As such they were disqualified.  A herd break for Scott McGregor and the UL-102 team.  

Did you know that the UL-102 is Charley Wiggins former High Pressure hull?  Scott acquired the hull from the Wiggins family to race with the fleet last year.

5:32 PM update...

Here is your order of finish for this years HYDRO-PROP Chrysler Jeep APBA Gold Cup!  The UL-72 Budweiser/Pocket Mechanic takes the win.  It was Charley Wiggins in the UL-10 Earl's Performance Products and Randy Haas in the UL-110 Miss CompAir at the start with a solid deck-to-deck fight.  Phil Bononcini and the UL-72 was back off the lead.  This worked to his advantage as both the UL-10 and the UL-110 jumped the gun and were penalized a lap.  Shortly after the announcement of the penalty, the UL-110 Miss CompAir blew an engine and went down in the water.
