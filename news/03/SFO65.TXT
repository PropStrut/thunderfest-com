THE CHRYSLER JEEP SUPERSTORES APBA GOLD CUP   August 21-24, 2003


*** THE CHRYSLER JEEP SUPERSTORES APBA GOLD CUP - IT'S OFFICIAL!

As many of you may have seen on the news or read in the paper
recently, the Mayor of Detroit held a press conference Tuesday
to make it official - the race is on!  A new organization is
being formed to produce the Gold Cup event this year.

New stories in the local papers include:
  http://www.detnews.com/2003/motorsports/0307/08/a01-212546.htm
  http://biz.yahoo.com/prnews/030708/detu005_1.html
  http://www.detnews.com/2003/motorsports/0307/09/e02-213441.htm

Those that are interested in volunteering will be able to do so
soon... signup begins this weekend at the Detroit River - River
Cruise, possibly under Pit Tower.

Ticket sales will begin very soon, perhaps as soon as Monday.

Contact info will be available very soon as well.

Keep an eye on http://www.gold-cup.com for more details as plans
develop.


*** REMINDER: JULY 11-13TH: DETROIT RIVER - RIVER CRUISE

The DRRC is a celebration to honor the Legends of Powerboat
History.  From Classic Gar Wood / Chris-Craft / Hacker Craft
runabouts to a flotilla of live entertainment, DJ's and concert
acts.

In addition, the Unlimited Lights mark their return to the
Detroit River with exhibition racing.

Party Central is the Roostertail Restaurant and the surrounding
area including Sindbad's Restaurant and the Dodge Pits.  For
more info see:
  http://www.detroitrivercruise.com
Friday is "Free Day". Advance tickets for adults are $15 on
Saturday and Sunday, at the gate tickets are $20.  A Family
package of 4 adult tickets and 8 children tickets is $50 and
unused tickets can be transferred to friends.


-- Brian Reed
