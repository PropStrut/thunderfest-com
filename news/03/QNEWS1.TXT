
Hello to everyone that has inquired about the race since
January...

I have been informed that the Detroit race WILL NOT be held
in June.  Apparently, SODT was unable to secure the necessary
sponsorship funding.  It is possible that a race will be run
later in the summer.  More information should be available
next week.

When I do have more information, I will send out one more email
to those of you that have inquired directly.  After that, I
won't send any more emails to this list.  For those of you that
do wish to continue receiving news about Detroit hydro racing,
please subscribe to our Signal Flag Online newsletter at the
top of our news page:
  http://www.thunderfest.com/news/index.html
