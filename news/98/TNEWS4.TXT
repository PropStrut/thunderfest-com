 CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT APBA GOLD CUP   July 9-12, 1998

Speaking of clock starts..  it's July and the clock is ticking
for the 1998 Gold Cup event!  This is a list of the Internet
plans we're working on:

 * News: On our web page each day.. results & the top stories.

 * ThunderCam: A camera atop Dodge Pit Tower to provide fans on
    the 'net with a glimpse of the pit activities.  July 9-12.

 * Webcast: WDIV will bring their LIVE TV coverage of the Gold
    Cup final to the 'net on Sunday (www.wdiv.com, 2:30 EDT).

For fans in Waterworks Park: look for the ThunderCam view within
the Carpenter Communications tent.

All links will be available on our home page next week - don't
miss out on the action!

- Brian Reed  (Volunteer WebMaster: www.thunderfest.com)
