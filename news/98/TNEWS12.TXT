 CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT APBA GOLD CUP   July 9-12, 1998


I've posted many photos from Friday, Saturday and Sunday.  The
direct URL is: "www.thunderfest.com/photogal/98/pits.html".


A scoring correction for the Inflatables from Kevin Gilday ..

 There was some confusion in the finals.   Boat 72 spit out its
 riders in the final lap and 16, 27 and 25 stopped (supposed to
 be an auto red flag) the other boats were coming out of the
 third turn and didn't see the spin.  Both of the guys from 72
 are ok but were required to take a ride in the rescue boat.

 The 27, 16 and 25 were in the Bandit class and improperly
 scored, that happens when you have two classes running
 together.  For info the official results (without the times):
  #1  16  Mike Christiani and James Christiani
  #2  27  Beth Gilday and Carol Urban (yep an all female team)
  #3  25  Andy Sallee and L. Winfield
 [Originally shown as DNFs, I gather that this Bandit class was
  scored by their position at the time of the red flag.]


For those in the Detroit area, I am pretty sure the Sylvan Lake
hydroplane races are this upcoming Sunday, July 19th.  I went
last year and had a great time.  No admission fee.  Get a map
out and find Sylvan Lake a bit northwest of Bloomfield... enter
the subdivision at Telegraph & Orchard Lake Roads.  Street
parking and you'll have to arrive early or walk a bit.. please
respect the local neighborhood and don't park where you shouldn't.
