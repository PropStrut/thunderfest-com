 Spirit Of Detroit Thunderfest - THUNDER NEWS #1 (June-15-98)
 ---------------------------------------------------------------
 CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT APBA GOLD CUP   July 9-12, 1998

Hello everyone and welcome to the new SODT Email newsletter.
We're just getting revved up but I wanted to let you know that
your Email subscription has been received.

Our first news is from a few months back:  Chrysler Jeep has
renewed their 96-98 title sponsorship through 2001!  As a fan
myself, I'm excited to find this level of commitment from a
world-class sponsor.  For those of you that haven't been able to
attend during Chrysler's sponsorship.. I just wish you could see.

While we rev up, don't forget:

 * The latest addition to our WWW Photo Gallery - dozens of
   great photos straight from Fred Alter's scrapbooks.  These
   are "must see" pictures from the 50's through the 70's.
   Click on the first link in our Gallery.

 * Friday (July 10) is FREE day in the Waterworks Park
   bleachers.  What better way to introduce a friend to the
   the sport?!  Friday's feature is the Automotive Gold Cup race
   between the Unlimited Lights and the Grand Prix boats (~5pm).

I'd like to hear from each of you - what do you want to hear
about in this newsletter?  Also, be sure let me know when you
have info that should appear here or on our web site.  Soon I'll
post information regarding related events.

- Brian Reed  (Volunteer WebMaster: www.thunderfest.com)
