Comments from Dave Villwock:

* Today went really good.. the water was perfect in the AM and
a little more choppy later.  It gave us a chance to adjust the
boat different ways and we were impressed with our late day
acceleration runs (over 160mph lap times).

* About his competitors: Appian and Llumar are really doing
well, PICO is sorta stuck and struggling a bit.

* The Detroit course is always changing - there can be a few
moguls in the turns you have to watch out for.  Anyone can run
when the water is perfect - what matters here in Detroit is how
well you run in the rough stuff.

* About props: We have quite a range of props that we use -
15" & 16" props, with different types of pitches from 24 to
30 inches.
