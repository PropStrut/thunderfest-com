One notable moment today was when Mark Tate had the closest call
(no double entendre was originally intended) with the Waterworks
seawall that I have EVER known of.  I thought an incident 2
years ago was close but it pales in comparison.  For those
unfamiliar with 'the point', it's a concrete wall on the outside
of the exit of the Roostertail turn - the tightest turn on the
UHRA circuit.  There is a bouy 10 feet off the point and Tate
came out of the turn with the bouy on his LEFT sponson!  Most
people couldn't believe the boat came out without a scratch.
No-one will ever come closer without consequences.  Anyone
have a photo for us to post? (credit & copyright will be
acknowledged!)

Also important is a half-note about Steve Weber.  During a race
today in the UL Pegasus / Bad Frog he was injured to some degree
in an incident that was not apparent to observers.  News was not
available even hours later, but I overheard that it may have had
to do with water in the cockpit.  I assume ULs have a bottom
hatch and perhaps it blew open...  if you know more, please tell
me so everyone can know.
