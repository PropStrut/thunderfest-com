 CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT APBA GOLD CUP   July 9-12, 1998

Another great and very sunny day here in the pits.  While the
day started out somewhat uneventfully, the afternoon brought
with it a number of news items..

FINAL QUALIFYING RESULTS:

* U101 PICO American Dream '98   N. Mark Evans/Mark Tate 148.934
* U99  R.S.EASTIN/BELL FORKLIFT  Rick Christensen 147.828

* U3   MASTER TIRE               Mark Weber       128.051#
* U100 PICO American Dream '96   N. Mark Evans    130.000*

Notes:
# The U3 is a piston-powered boat and qualifies at 120 or above.
+ Denotes increased qualification speed
* Denotes N2 violation

For U101 Mark Evans qualified the boat, and in the afternoon
a rumor shot through pit lane - Mark Tate was taking it out to
see if he felt comfortable in it!  I think he had a 139mph lap
time and he _will_ be driving this boat for the weekend.

I heard U8 had a problem at the end of the day.. I was behind
the grandstands as he was going by at a good pace then a sharp
noise and the motor went completely quiet - too quiet.  I heard
that the prop shaft broke and may have damaged the boat
severely.  I'm sure we all wish them well for speedy repairs!
