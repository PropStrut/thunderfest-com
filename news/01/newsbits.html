[07/14/01 11:01] : By Brian Reed
MORE BAD LUCK
During morning practice, Llumar Window Films had an incident identical to the SHelby/DYC problem yesterday. The prop broke in the Roostertail turn, ruining the strut and the 'shaft log' (aka stuffing box?) ... the tube on the bottom of the hull that the shaft goes through. No word yet if they'll try to repair it here, but I don't think they hurt the hull itself as much as the Shelby/DYC did.

BAD LUCK IN THE FINAL QUALIFYING SESSION
First Llumar blew something near the gearbox - hopefully not the box itself, then the Shelby/DYC was rounding the Roostertail turn when we heard a big crunch - they lost a blade on the prop and now have a hole at least a foot around in the bottom of the hull. They're looking at it now but are doubtful about being ready for the Saturday racing. There will be no qualifying on Saturday, testing only. The course is now officially closed.
