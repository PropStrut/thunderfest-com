THE CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT *APBA GOLD CUP*       July 12-15, 2001


*** OUR FIRST SFO ISSUE OF 2001

Hello and a belated "Happy New Year!" to all our readers.  

During the first few days of February, Hydro-Prop held it's
annual meeting and awards ceremonies in St. Louis.  Spirit Of
Detroit Thunderfest is proud to be the recipient of the
"Management Team of the Year" award for 2000, and we want you
to know that planning for Gold Cup 2001 is well underway.


*** GEORGE STRATTON MEMORIAL BENEFIT - MARCH 24TH AT THE DYC

Please join us for an auction to benefit the George Stratton
Memorial Trust, an educational fund that has been established
for his children Christopher and Natalia.  Master of ceremonies
N. Mark Evans will join auctioneer Jerry Toner for the live
auction, and there will be a silent auction as well.

Reservations are available now, and the list of auction items
is fabulous.  Mentioning just a few ... Jersey Speed Skiff "Ride
of Your Life"; Autographed helmets and driver suits; Seating
packages for the Gold Cup race; Luncheons with Racing Legends;
Vintage boat ride; Offshore boat ride; and so much more.

The event will be held at the Detroit Yacht Club on March 24th,
and your hosts are SODT, Unlimiteds Detroit, Marine Prop Riders,
Detroit Yacht Club and USA Racing Partners.

Unlimiteds Detroit has a web page with all the details:
  http://www.unlimitedsdetroit.com/stratton/


*** REMEMBERING THE BEST

At the Gold Cup race, George powered into third place overall
and beat Dave Villwock (in Miss Budweiser) and teammate Mark
Weber (in Miss York/DYC) to the J. Lee Schoenith award.  The
J. Lee Schoenith is a prestigious award itself, going to the
driver with the fastest overall speed as a tribute to the
original method of determining the Gold Cup winner.

A new addition to our photo gallery is a picture of Appian
Jeronimo clearing the seawall, capturing a moment that so many
of us remember:
  http://www.thunderfest.com/photogal/00/white/jeronimo.jpg
Thanks to Paul T. White, and look for his donation of a print
in the benefit's silent auction.

As the season was coming to a close, it was pretty obvious that
George would become Hydros' "Rookie Of The Year".  In his honor,
the award has been renamed the "George Stratton Rookie of the
Year Award".


*** MOVIE DAY AT THE DOSSIN - "BEHIND THE WHEEL" - MARCH 17TH

On Saturday March 17th, the Great Lakes Maritime Institute and
Unlimiteds Detroit present "Behind The Wheel", a movie day
focusing on the drivers.  The movies start about Noon, with
swapping and "show and tell" beginning at 11am.  The Dossin
Museum is at location #5 on our Belle Isle map:
  http://www.thunderfest.com/spectate/bimap2.html
First time visitors note that these are one-way streets... it's
easiest to stay to the right (follow the shoreline around the
Scott Fountain #7) until you get to the Dossin.


*** "MADISON" UNVEILED AT SUNDANCE FILM FESTIVAL

In January, the movie "Madison" made it's public debut at the
Sundance Film Festival and received a standing ovation.  Most of
you already know that the movie stars Jim Caviezel and Jake
Lloyd in a father/son story that incorporates the excitement of
the 1971 Miss Madison Gold Cup win in Madison, Indiana.
  Movie web-site (Flash): http://www.madisonthemovie.com
  Sundance Film Festival: http://www.sundance.org/reg
The Hydroplane and Raceboat Museum has a review and more links
on their site (look about halfway down the page):
  http://www.thunderboats.org
