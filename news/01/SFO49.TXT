*** THE UNLIMITED QUALIFYING RESULTS AND NEWS
By Bruce Madej
Associated Press

DETROIT (AP)---Mark Weber, St. Clair Shores, Mich., was just
getting revved up to be the fastest qualifier for the Chrysler
Jeep APBA Gold Cup.  Just then, misfortune raised its ugly head.
Weber's boat threw a blade of the propeller, wreaking havoc on
the craft on its final qualifying attempt, knocking the U-15
Emcor presents the Detroiit Yacht Club out of the Gold Cup.
"We threw an ear off the propeller, and the shaft then goes out
of balance, pushing it upwards toward the gearbox," said Weber.
"The crew is tired and we have major damage to the boat."  Dave
Villwock, Seattle, Wash., in the Miss Budweiser created the
battle for the top qualifying slot when he pushed the Miss
Budweiser to a two-lap leading average of 154.135 mph, edging
Mike Hanson, Kent, Wash., in the U-9 Tubby's Grilled Submarine
boat.  Hanson had the best time of the day until Villwock took
the spot away, creating another opportunity for Weber to grab
the top spot.  But Weber had the problem in turn four on his
warm up lap.  "We had something special going with this boat,"
added Weber.  "Now, we'll have to use the other boat and that is
not a bad boat, but we had something going with the other boat."
    With the U-15 out of the race, ten boats will now compete
for the Gold Cup championship this weekend. Weber's misfortune
now knocks his brother Mike out of the cockpit as Mark will
drive the second team boat, the U-10, Shelby Enterprises
presents the Miss DYC.  On Friday night, Dave Bender, El Dorado,
Calif., won his first Automotive Powered Gold Cup Friday in the
Miss Security Race Products, averaging  84.306 mph.
