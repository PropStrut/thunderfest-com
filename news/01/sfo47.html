*** EVANSVILLE RESULTS

The Hydros visited Evansville Indiana on June 30th and July 1st, and the winner was .... Dave Villwock in the Miss Budweiser. Second went to Mike Hanson in the U-9 Miss WABX, third to Greg Hopp in Miss Znetix, fourth to Detroiter Mark Weber in the U-10 Emcor, and fifth to Detroiter Jimmy King in the U-8 Llumar Window Film.

New rules for 2001 intended to increase the competition seem to have had some good effect, reports are that the racing was much closer. Mike Hanson was the top qualifier at 154.179, thus ending Miss Budweiser's streak of being the top qualifier in 28 consecutive Hydroplane races.

By all accounts, the U-9 is faaast this year!

*** STATUS REPORT OF THE 'MADISON' MOVIE BY DAVID WILLIAMS

I've been working on the Madison movie one way or another since the summer of 1993. It is a slow and frustrating project that moves at a blistering pace for a few weeks and then there are months and months on inaction.

The movie does have a distributor. The name of the company cannot be released yet. The movie was set for an Oct 12th opening in about 2000 theaters nation wide. However in a "good news bad news" situation, we have learned the Madison star Jim Caviezel has another movie, the major studio release "Count of Monte Cristo" scheduled to also open on Oct 12. It is not a good idea to open two movies with the same star on the same weekend. That tends to split the potential audience, so a decision has been made to hold Madison until after "The Count of Monte Cristo" opens (maybe in spring 2002). If the "Count" is a good as the previews say, it should really be a boost to "our" movie.

When the movie is released, there is supposed to be a $15,000,000 promotional campaign to announce it, so . . . when the distributor wants us all to know more, we will all know about it. Until then, please be patient and keep waiting.

David Williams
Raceboat Coordinator for Madison
