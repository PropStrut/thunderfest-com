SIGNAL FLAG ONLINE #23

CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT APBA GOLD CUP July 8-11, 1999
Thursday July 8 1999

*** THURSDAY

The 1999 Gold Cup races have begun! Another excellent day on the Detroit River and some great qualifying runs were made. Today was Media Welcome day, and boats from all classes ran exhibition laps in the afternoon. Four Unlimiteds joined the 'field': PICO, Bud, Chrysler/Jeep (U-2), and Young Country / DYC (U-10). This brought the crowd to their feet in anticipation of this weekend's action.
