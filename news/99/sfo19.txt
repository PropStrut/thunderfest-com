Spirit Of Detroit Thunderfest - SIGNAL FLAG ONLINE #19 (May 16)
---------------------------------------------------------------
THE CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT *APBA GOLD CUP*      July 8-11, 1999


*** IN THIS ISSUE:

* Ticket plan changes for 1999.  Waterworks Park Annex returns
    to General Admission with waterfront blanket viewing, and
    Pit Corner returns to bleacher seating.

* The 1999 T'fest raffle features a personal watercraft, child's
    snowmobile and a Prowler go-cart.

* Thunderfest Group Sales campaign takes off.


*** #4 WATERWORKS PARK ANNEX RETURNS TO GENERAL ADMISSION!

The grandstand seats from the Waterworks Park Annex have been
removed to make way for the newly designed general admission
style viewing area.  This area will now feature waterfront
blanket viewing, a huge general admission tent, live
entertainment, corporate tents, displays, concessions, a new
and improved bridge to Waterworks Park and more plans underway.

For adults it's $15 on Saturday or $25 on Sunday ($35 for both).
Ages 7-10 are $5, $10 and $10 respectively.  Ages 6 and under free.


*** PIT CORNER VIEWING AREA HAS NEW & IMPROVED DESIGN

Pit Corner spectators will have even a better view this year as
Thunderfest replaces the theater-style padded seats with an
increased amount of bleacher seats.  The price is $125 per
reserved seat, which includes Gold Cup Pit Passes, parking for
Saturday and Sunday and a subscription to the Signal Flag
newsletter.  You also may purchase the Sunday catered lunch
package, which is $150 per seat.  These seats are on sale now
and will sell out quick, so call Thunderfest at 800.359.7760
today to order!


*** THUNDERFEST GROUP SALES CAMPAIGN TAKES OFF!

A major Group Sales campaign was launched in late November to
attract more companies to the unique corporate entertaining
opportunities at the Chrysler Jeep APBA Gold Cup.  Groups of 25
or more will receive a special discount on tickets this year!
Some of the amenities include grandstand viewing, picnic-style
viewing, pit access, special attractions and displays, live
entertainment and much, much more!  Thunderfest will provide
your group or company with an exciting presentation about the
event upon request.  For the detailed Group Sales info
contact Jennifer Hayes at 800.359.7760, M-F 9 a.m. -5 p.m.


*** TICKETS ONLY $20 FOR 1999 SODT RAFFLE!

  1ST PLACE - PERSONAL WATERCRAFT
  2ND PLACE - CHILD'S SNOWMOBILE
  3RD PLACE - PROWLER GO-CART

Proceeds benefit Spirit of Detroit Thunderfest and Special
Olympics, Michigan.
