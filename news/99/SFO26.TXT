THE CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT *APBA GOLD CUP*      July 8-11, 1999


*** CHIP HANAUER IN PICO FLAT-OUT BEATS DAVE VILLWOCK IN BUD!

The excitement the fans showed during the final heat was really
unbelievable!  As the boats approached the line for the start,
it was clear that Dave wasn't where he wanted to be - a bit
behind and between two other boats.  In one motion everyone
stood up and started hollering and clapping, and the clamor
grew until Chip was done with the TV interviews.

The largest crowd in memory stayed to see the Gold Cup Trophy
presentation, chanting CHIP CHIP CHIP and PICO PICO PICO.  I
can still taste the excitement in the air!

Coming off the first Belle Isle bridge turn, PICO was almost a
roostertail ahead of Bud, a lead which he lengthened to about
three by the finish.  Both were racing hard - floating the
front end and tipping from sponson to sponson.

Hanauer has now won three of the five UHRA events this year.
Chip will tell you that Dave brings more to a team with
technical skills (prop, engine, etc); but I'll say that when it
comes to driving, Hanauer is the maestro.

*** CHIP'S STATS

- Hanauer is one race win away from Muncey's record of 62.  He
notes that he is indifferent about breaking Bill's record, and
in my opinion it seemed that while of course he wants to
continue winning, he doesn't want to take Bill's name off this
record.  Chip went on to say that of all the people really close
to him, Fran Muncey has been the only one that has remained
adamant that he return to racing and go after that record.
It was a touching moment during the press interview.

- Since 1982, Chip has started in 14 Gold Cups, making it to 13
final heats and winning an unprecendented 11 Gold Cups.  Six
of them were here in Detroit.  Wow!!

- Hanauer is the last driver to win the Gold Cup with piston
power (Atlas Van Lines 1983-Evansville OH) and also the first
driver to win the Gold Cup with turbine power (AVL 1984-Pasco
WA).  Wow!!

*** WE ALL HAVE OTHER INTERESTS

I know you're all racing fans - while the Paris-Ruebiex
event is my favorite bicycle race, the Tour de France is their
Gold Cup.  Lance Armstrong (United States) has won the 8th stage
of the TdF in addition to winning the pre-TdF trial stage.  Not
unlike Chip he has been out for a few years, but in Lance's case
it was because of recovery from cancer.  If you haven't picked
your favorite for the TdF yet, choose Lance and stay tuned to
the race coverage.

Mentioning Lance makes it impossible to not mention the U.S.
Women's Soccer team win.  Saturday night I was at Sindbad's
with the boating crowd and suddenly we were a soccer crowd too.
