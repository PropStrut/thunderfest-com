*** BIG BOY 5 LITRE NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP

The first start included 10 boats, but with an incident in the Roostertail turn, it was red flagged. Only 5 boats were able to attempt the re-start. In the incident, one boat hit a hole and hooked the boat hard. A second boat right next to it became involved but I think it was a soft 'hit' because of their proximity. A third boat came along and ran into the first two pretty hard. All drivers were OK, though one was kept under observation for an hour or so. Sorry, I didn't get the names.
