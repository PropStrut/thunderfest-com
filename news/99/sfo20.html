SIGNAL FLAG ONLINE #20

CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT APBA GOLD CUP July 8-11, 1999
Monday June 21 1999

*** THE BARRIE (CANADA) RACE - JUNE 20TH

Dave Villwock in Miss Bud takes the checkered flag, with Chip Hanauer in Miss PICO about 3 roostertails behind. Chip started out front but Dave powered by during the first lap. Third was Steve David (Freddie's Club), then Greg Hopp (United Furniture) and Detroiter Mike Weber (Cooper's Express).

Todd Yarling (Miss Madison) and Mark Weber (Mike's brother in York International) flipped over in separate incidents, the good news is that both are OK. Todd reports a soft landing while Mark has a sore foot and went for an xray. Everyone hopes that both teams are ready for the next race.

Unlimited Light Final:
1st - UL10 (Earl's)
2nd - UL16 (Alamo)
3rd - UL72 (Pocket Mechanic)
DNS - UL110 (Comp Air)

*** UNVEILING OF THE NEW JIM CLARY PAINTING - THIS THURSDAY!

Jim Clary and special guests will be present to unveil "His Most Exciting Race", the second painting in Jim's 4 year series. Everyone is welcome - $5 admission. DON'T MISS IT! Thursday June 24th 6pm at the Detroit Yacht Club on Belle Isle.

*** MEETING FOR SODT VOLUNTEERS - NEXT TUESDAY (JUNE 29TH)

This is the yearly mandatory meeting - June 29th in Pontiac MI at the Silverdome's "Main Event" restaurant. 6pm. Free to paid volunteers, guests are $10. This is the time to pick up your volunteer credentials, tshirts, cap, etc.

*** UNLIMITEDS DETROIT 30TH ANNIVERSARY MEETING & PARTY

Come party with Detroit's most dedicated hydro fans after the course closes on Wednesday July 7th, at the Detroit Yacht Club. I will see you there! 6pm to 9pm - Cash bar - Munchies. Limited space, RSVP to Hank Kosciusszko at 313.886.8608.
