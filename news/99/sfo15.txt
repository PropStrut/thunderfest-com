*** T'FEST Volunteer Spring Gathering (This Thursday)

This is a new event in the yearly calendar.  It's just for fun,
not a mandatory meeting.  Thursday April 22 6-9pm, Bruce Post
VFW Hall, 11.5 Mile & Jefferson, St Clair Shores.  Fun, food,
beverages, door prizes and all the latest Thunderfest and team
info.  Dues increased to $50 April 1, but if you pay at the door
then we'll give you the pre-April rate of $40.  First time
volunteers get the best rate anytime of year.


*** USS Grayfox at the Great Lakes Maritime Institute (Saturday)

The GLMI and Dossin Museum invite you to come aboard the USS
Grayfox this Saturday (April 24).  An exclusive trip for *new*
GLMI members only.  11am to 12noon... the Grayfox will be docked
at the GLMI 12-4pm for the rest of us.  Space is limited!

 MUSEUM VISITORS MAY TOUR THE DOCKED BOAT AT NO CHARGE. DON'T
