Spirit Of Detroit Thunderfest - SIGNAL FLAG ONLINE #31 (Oct 24)
---------------------------------------------------------------
THE CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT *APBA GOLD CUP*        July 6-9, 2000


THIS ISSUE: NEWS FROM THE 33RD ANNUAL FINISH / START PARTY.
The Finish / Start party is our official rollover from one
year's efforts to the next.


*** YEAR 2000 RACE DATE ANNOUNCED:

Get out your calendars!  The next APBA GOLD CUP event will be
held July 6th through July 9th, 2000.


*** MARK WEBER SPOKE ABOUT THE KIM GREGORY TEAM:

He said that the team has aquired the services of some of the
Appian Jeronimo crew members for next year and that Jerry Rice
may assist also.  This puts a little weight behind the rumor
that the Appian team may disband, though it was emphasized no
equipment has changed hands.  


*** SODT IS LOOKING FOR NEW VOLUNTEERS

Our goal is 60 to 80 more for year 2000.  We will be awarding
two round-trip airline tickets to the member who recruits the
most new volunteers!  Be sure to note your name on their
application for credit.


*** 1999 VOLUNTEER AWARDS:

-- A NUMBER OF VERY SPECIAL AWARDS WERE PRESENTED:

  The prestigious Jack Adams Special Achievement award is
  awarded to volunteers that go above and beyond the call of
  duty year after year.  This year it was presented to
  MICHAEL AUBREY by Jack Love.  Congratulations!

  A Lifetime Achievement award was presented to TOM PAVEL for
  his contributions spanning nearly 50 years.  A boisterous
  'roast' followed.

  A Memorial award was presented to the family of RONALD
  GABRIEL.  Ron was a rescue boat driver that passed away
  earlier this year.
