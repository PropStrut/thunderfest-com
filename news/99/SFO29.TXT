THE CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT *APBA GOLD CUP*      July 8-11, 1999


*** RECENT RACES: NORFOLK & TRI-CITIES

Since the Gold Cup race, Dave Villwock in Miss Budweiser tied
Chip Hanauer in Miss PICO for 3 wins on the season at Norfolk,
then went on to make it 4-3 at the Tri-Cities (WA) event.

During the Tri-Cities event, Chip rode up a roostertail and
flipped the Miss PICO.  Word is that this was just a racing
incident, he wasn't cut off.  The rescue crews did their job and
took all the proper precautions, putting him in a neck brace for
a trip to the hospital for x-rays which were all negative.  The
hull was repaired for the final heat and Mike Weber drove Miss
PICO to a second place finish!  The Leland camp reports that
Chip will be ready for the Seattle race.


*** DETROITER MARK TATE: AT THE WHEEL AGAIN

Mark Tate has signed on as the U-2 Harvey Motorsports driver,
relieving Steve David who has left the position sooner than
expected to spend more time with his growing business.  Go Mark!
