Spirit Of Detroit Thunderfest - SIGNAL FLAG ONLINE #20 (Jun 21)

THE CHRYSLER JEEP DETROIT *APBA GOLD CUP*      July 8-11, 1999


*** THE BARRIE (CANADA) RACE - JUNE 20TH

Dave Villwock in Miss Bud takes the checkered flag, with Chip
Hanauer in Miss PICO about 3 roostertails behind.  Chip started
out front but Dave powered by during the first lap.  Third was
Steve David (Freddie's Club), then Greg Hopp (United Furniture)
and Detroiter Mike Weber (Cooper's Express).

Todd Yarling (Miss Madison) and Mark Weber (Mike's brother in
York International) flipped over in separate incidents, the
good news is that both are OK.  Todd reports a soft landing
while Mark has a sore foot and went for an xray.  Everyone hopes
that both teams are ready for the next race.

Unlimited Light Final:
 1st - UL10 (Earl's)
 2nd - UL16 (Alamo)
 3rd - UL72 (Pocket Mechanic)
 DNS - UL110 (Comp Air)
