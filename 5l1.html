5 Litre Hydroplanes

Spirit of Detroit THUNDERFEST is pleased to welcome the 5 Litre class to our event for the first time!

The 5 Liter hydroplanes are one of the most exciting and competitive classes in the APBA. These racers go all out and sometimes there's even a little "bumping and banging" as they race deck-to-deck.

5 Litre hydroplanes are a minimum of 16 feet in length, and must weight at least 1450 pounds (with the driver). They are powered to speeds over 100 MPH by mostly unmodified 305 Chevy or 302 Ford engines. They must run 2 barrel carburetors and standard 'pump' gasoline.

These boats are slightly smaller then the Unlimited Light Hydroplanes. A reinforced cockit is mandatory, full capsules are optional.

Past 5 Litre National Champions include Chip Hanauer, Mark Weber, Mike Weber, Steve Weber, Bo Schide and Mark Tate.
