$20 Gold Cup Pit Pass

The $20 Gold Cup Pit Pass gets you close to the raceboats in the main Dodge Pits paddock area. This pass is good for Thursday and Friday (the qualifying days), and includes a seat in the Waterworks Park bleachers on those days. For ticket holders to Plan #1, #2, #3, #4, #5 and #6, this pass will get you into the pits on Saturday until 11am.

Don't forget the bleachers are a great place to watch the highlight of Friday's action, the Automotive Gold Cup race.

For parking information, see the Dodge Pits Parking - Qualifying Days page.
