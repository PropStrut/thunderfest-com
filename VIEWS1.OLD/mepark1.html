Memorial Annex Parking

Memorial Annex (Plan #7) parking is in nearby lots, with a nominal parking fee.

To get to this area, turn off Jefferson onto Parkview Drive. Parkview Drive is 1.25 miles East of the Belle Isle (MacArthur) Bridge at Grand Boulevard, and about 1.5 miles West of Conner. Look at the map of the ticket areas and note that the other streets in this area will not allow incoming traffic during the event. Parkview has a visible sign on Jefferson, and look for a large yellow brick building on the corner.

Follow Parkview Drive to the end, turn right on Dwight and follow it 3 blocks ahead. Memorial Annex is at this corner, and a THUNDERFEST volunteer will assist you in finding the best available spot.
