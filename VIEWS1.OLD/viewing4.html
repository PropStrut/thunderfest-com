What To Bring and Wear

Wear comfortable walking shoes and bring suntan lotion! You may also wish to bring a small cap to shade your face, and/or sunglasses.

If you're seated on the beach or grass or in a bleacher area, bring a blanket. They double as a good bleacher pad.

Due to how quickly weather can change in Detroit - choose among your t-shirts, sweaters, windbreakers or jackets when leaving for the event. Remember that even a small breeze off the cool river makes this area a few degrees cooler than the outlying cities. We usually have spectacular spring days for our event, but it's a good idea to be prepared.

14" beverage coolers are allowed in the General Admission areas #7, #8 and #9. NO glass or carry-in alcohol (see policy below).

You may want to bring your radio, as our official radio stations will be broadcasting race info during the day.

THUNDERFEST POLICY

SECURITY PERSONNEL HAVE THE RIGHT TO EVICT ANYONE IN VIOLATION OF THESE RESTRICTIONS!

  • NO carry-in alcohol.
  • NO glass.
  • NO Tailgating.
  • NO picnics in parking areas.
  • ALL coolers are subject to search.
  • NO containers larger than 14" any dimension.
  • No containers of any size permitted on seats.
  • Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.
  • Absolutely NO SMOKING in the Dodge or Belle Isle pits. This is a NO TOLERANCE rule. (Jet-A and methanol fuels are stored throughout pit lane!)
  • NO alcoholic beverages are permitted into the pit compounds until the river has closed to racing for the day! Again, no tolerance.
