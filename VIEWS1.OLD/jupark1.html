Judges Stand / VIP Stockton Parking

For Plans #5 and #6, reserved parking is behind the viewing areas. These areas require a Spirit Of Detroit Judges Stand or VIP Stockton Parking pass! One parking pass is included with each 4 tickets purchased for these areas. Additional parking passes may be purchased on a limited basis for Judges Stand ($30) and VIP Stockton ($20).

These areas are located on Parkview Drive, near Jefferson. Parkview Drive is 1.25 miles East of the Belle Isle (MacArthur) Bridge at Grand Boulevard, and about 1.5 miles West of Conner. Look at the map of the ticket areas and note that the other streets in this area will not allow incoming traffic during the event. Parkview has a visible sign on Jefferson, and look for a large yellow brick building on the corner. Follow Parkview Drive to the end and a THUNDERFEST volunteer will assist you in finding a suitable spot for your vehicle.

Extra parking is available in Henderson Park for a nominal on-site fee, and there is a free shuttle to these areas. Henderson Park is located on Jefferson nearly a mile East of the Belle Isle (MacArthur) Bridge at Grand Boulevard, and a bit less than 2 miles West of Conner.
