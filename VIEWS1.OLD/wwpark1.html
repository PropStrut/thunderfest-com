Waterworks Park Parking

For Plans #1, #2, #3 and #4, reserved parking is inside Waterworks Park. This area requires a Spirit Of Detroit Waterworks Park Parking pass on Saturday and Sunday! One parking pass is included with each 4 tickets for these areas and they are also available on a limited basis for $20.

Waterworks Park is located on Jefferson, about 1.5 miles East of the Belle Isle (MacArthur) Bridge at Grand Boulevard, and a bit more than 1 mile West of Conner. This is a large gated field and is easy to find.

Extra parking is available in Henderson Park for a nominal on-site fee, and there is a free shuttle to this area. Henderson Park is located on Jefferson nearly a mile East of the Belle Isle (MacArthur) Bridge at Grand Boulevard, and a bit less than 2 miles West of Conner.
