Dodge Pits Parking - Qualifying Days

For qualifying day Pit Passes, Pit Tours and the Budget Pass, parking is behind the Dodge Pits in Muncey Park. There is an on-site parking fee.

To get to this area, turn off Jefferson onto Marquette Drive. Marquette Drive is 1.5 miles East of the Belle Isle (MacArthur) Bridge at Grand Boulevard, and about 1.25 miles West of Conner. Marquette borders the East end of Waterworks Park which is a large gated field. Follow Marquette Drive to Muncey Park at the end, and a THUNDERFEST volunteer will assist you in finding the best available spot.

Extra parking is available in Henderson Park for a nominal on-site fee, and there is a free shuttle to this area. Henderson Park is located on Jefferson nearly a mile East of the Belle Isle (MacArthur) Bridge at Grand Boulevard, and a bit less than 2 miles West of Conner.
