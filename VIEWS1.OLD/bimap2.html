Belle Isle Landmarks

    Dossin Great Lakes Museum. Scale-model Great Lakes ships, maritime memorabilia and special hands-on exhibits make this museum entertaining for young and old alike. Open Wed. - Sun. 10-4 (Year Round).

    Aquarium and Whitcomb Conservatory. America's oldest Aquarium features freshwater stingrays and electric eels, and is a regular hit with children. The Conservatory is patterned after Monticello and has permanent displays of cacti, ferns, palms, and one of the largest collections of orchids in the country. Both of these buildings were designed by Albert Kahn. Open 10-5 daily, but the Conservatory is seasonal (Memorial Day to Late Sept.).

    Scott Memorial Fountain. This huge carved marble statue was designed by Cass Gilbert and has been called "America's Greatest Statue".

    Belle Isle Zoo. Built on 13 acres, the zoo is another regular favorite with the kids and features an elevated walkway for viewing. Treat yourself to a mini-safari 10-5 daily (May 1 - Oct 31).

    Nature Center. Information on Belle Isle's flora and fauna, and a nature trail that's handicapped accessible. Open Wed. - Sun. 10-4 (Year Round).
