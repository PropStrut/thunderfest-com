Henderson Park

This park is a general admission area.

A free Budget Pass is included with every Sunday ticket for access to the Waterworks Park bleachers on Thursday or Friday.

Bleacher and Picnic/BBQ areas are available on a first-come basis. You may bring in a 14" beverage cooler (no glass).

Parking for this area is on-site, with a nominal parking fee. This park is located on Jefferson nearly a mile East of the Belle Isle (MacArthur) Bridge at Grand Boulevard, and a bit less than 2 miles West of Conner. Henderson is a good size park with permanent signs, and is easy to find.
