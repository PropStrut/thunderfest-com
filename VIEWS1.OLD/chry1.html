Chrysler (Owen) Park

Chrysler Park is a general admission area. This park is closed on Saturday.

A free Budget Pass is included with every Sunday ticket for access to the Waterworks Park bleachers on Thursday or Friday.

Picnic/BBQ areas are available on a first-come basis. You may bring in a 14" beverage cooler (no glass).


(Photo copyright Joe Pfeiffer)

Parking for this area is on-site, with a nominal parking fee. This park is located at about 8431 Jefferson; 3/4 of a mile East of the Belle Isle (MacArthur) Bridge at Grand Boulevard, and about 2 miles West of Conner. There are no permanent park signs here, but there will be race signs posted in mid-May.
