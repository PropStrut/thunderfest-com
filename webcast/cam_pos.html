Hi Chris and Jim. This page is to show you all the good camera positions that I know of.

The yellow Area numbers are the spectator ticket plan #'s. For info about these areas (including photos), see the http://www.thunderfest.com Spectator Info pages.

The aqua circles are the camera positions, somewhat ordered from best to least. Circle #0 is the Dodge Pit Tower. This would be a good area for your central system, if we can fit you in.

The rest of the camera positions are explained below.

Aqua #1: Pit Corner. At the waters edge, just above river level. A very good spot.

Aqua #2: Corner of the far end of the bleacher area. A very good spot if I can get the OK for you to be here. If you used just one camera position, this would probably be it. Spectator shots are close by.

Aqua #3: The Belle Isle (aka "Daisy") pits. Most of the UL, JSS and 5-Liter boats will be here. The shot of the boats as they gun the engine to get on plane on their way out is great. Sample: Click on the 3rd link, "An AVI clip of the automotive powered Grand Prix boats ('96)". Actually, this is a shot from the other bank, but you get the idea. The second half of the video is taken from Pit Corner.

Aqua #4: The Detroit Yacht Club. The dot is positioned on the dock where you can get a shot of the boats coming up the backstretch. Also at the DYC docks will be a number of the Vintage boats. On Friday evening the DYC is host to the Hydrofest benefit party, it would be swell to cover this too.

Aqua #5: This is the Start / Finish line - Judges Stand. Real tight on space here, and I think Aqua #6 is better because from there you can get the checkered flag and the winner crossing the line in one shot.

Aqua #6: Stockton Park. See notes under #5. This area can also afford some good spectator shots.

Aqua #7: Memorial Annex. A small very comfy park with spectators.

Aqua #8: Henderson Park. General admission for the rock & roll crowd. (This park sponsored by the local R&R station)

Aqua #9: Owens Park. I think this is sponsored by an oldies station.

Aqua #10: The Belle Isle beach. Free viewing area with good crowd shots.

Aqua #11: At the start / finish line, I think this is a coast guard boat. It may not be a possible camera position, but I wanted to map it.

Aqua #12: Inside the Roostertail turn. These boat positions are demanding - the boats go out before the course opens and stay out ALL day. Some of the crew members already do their own video or photography and might do better than even a good pro videographer on his first trip out. For a sample, see the link in our Photo Gallery "MPEG Video" [under 1999 and "Miss Bud riding up a roostertail"]. This video was taken from this Aqua #12 position as the boats speed up for the start. It begins with a shot of judges stand.

Aqua #13: Inside the bridge turn. Is this a shot or is this a shot?!
