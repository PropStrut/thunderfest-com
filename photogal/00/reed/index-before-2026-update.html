The Chrysler Jeep Detroit APBA Gold Cup
'00 VIDEO BY BRIAN REED

This is a closeup shot of Miss Appian Jeronimo as it passes outside of the stalled Miss Elam Plus. It went by so fast that the incident is over in 8 frames of video (less than 1/3 a second). The frames shown are consecutive frames.

The first frame looks like the top of the canopy, the second the scoop, and the sixth the tail fin (yellow in the final heat using a spare York fin).
