1996 Collision Between
Miss Bud and Smokin' Joe's

These photo's were taken from inside the bridge turn
area from Tango 8, one of the rescue / tow boats.
Thanks to Bill Welser for the photo's.

Joe's starts into the Bridge turn.
Miss Bud doesn't turn well (bouncing on waves).
Miss Bud lost her footing
Scoots sideways on top of Smokin' Joe's.
Momentum takes her over,
her right side starts to come down...
Right side 'trips' on the water,
flipping the boat around violently.
