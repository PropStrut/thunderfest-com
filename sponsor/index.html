WE HAVE THE DEMOGRAPHICS YOU WANT:
  • THE RIGHT AGE AND OCCUPATION LEVEL:
    67% are 25-44 years of age, and 48% hold professional or managerial positions.
  • STEADY WAGE EARNERS:
    42% earn between $25K-$50K each year, and 29% more than $50K.
  • PREDOMINANTLY MALE:
    74% are male, and the majority are head of households with 1 or more children.
  • SUPER SPENDERS:
    A majority purchased an automobile, automotive parts and electronic equipment within the last 3 years. Most use credit cards and own their own homes.
  • BIG BIG SPENDERS AT THE RACE:
    Fans in our reserved seating areas spend an average of $40 on food and $50 on incidentals and souvenirs at the race.
STATISTICS PROVIDED BY THE MARKETING DEPARTMENT AT OREGON UNIVERSITY AND BY THE RESEARCH FIRM OF IRWIN, BROH & ASSOCIATES.
