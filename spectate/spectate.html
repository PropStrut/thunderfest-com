Spectator & Ticket Info 2002

Because our event covers a large area on the Detroit River there are many very different viewing areas and ticket plans, from a casual blanket picnic to exciting viewing at the water's edge. Feel free to call if you have any questions.

The best areas for young children and families are the general admission areas. Each of the paid viewing areas provide a full range of food, beer and wine for purchase (no alcohol within the pit areas). See the Rules & Regulations for info about bringing a cooler.

Viewing from Waterworks Grandstands is FREE on Thursday & Friday!
