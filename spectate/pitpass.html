Pit Passes

  • All Pit Passes are honored in the Dodge & Belle Isle Pits.
  • The parking fee is nominal, and there is a free Shuttle service.


Superfan Hot Pit Credential
Photo of the Hot Pit Area

Access to the Unlimited Hydroplane Launch Area in the Dodge Pits.
This is the only pass that gets you into this area.
Close enough to touch the boats, but only the crews can do that!
This pass is good for all 4 days.

  • $125 per credential or
  • $175 per credential with reserved seat in Plan #3
  • Liability waiver signature is required
  • Reserved parking for Thursday - Sunday


Race Day Pit Passes
Photo of Pit Area

Access to the Dodge Pit paddock on Saturday and Sunday (race days).

For ticket holders with reserved seats:

  • Saturday - $20 per pass
  • Sunday - $20 per pass
  • Saturday & Sunday - $30 per pass
Non-Ticket holders:
  • Saturday - $20 per pass
  • Sunday - $50 per pass


Qualifying Day Pit Passes
Photo of Pit Area

Access to the Dodge Pit paddock on Thursday or Friday for time trials with a non-reserved seat in the Waterworks Park grandstands

A VIP Pit Pass is good for one qualifying day (Thursday or Friday), while the Gold Cup Pit Pass is good for both qualifying days.

  • VIP Pit Pass - $10
  • Gold Cup Pit Pass - $20
