The Chrysler Jeep Detroit APBA Gold Cup
Rules and Regulations

SECURITY PERSONNEL HAVE THE RIGHT TO EVICT ANYONE IN VIOLATION OF THESE RESTRICTIONS!

  • NO carry-in alcohol.
  • NO glass.
  • NO Tailgating.
  • NO picnics in parking areas.
  • ALL coolers are subject to search.
  • NO containers larger than 14" any dimension.
  • No containers of any size permitted on seats.
  • Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.
  • NO alcoholic beverages are permitted into the pit compounds until the river has closed to racing for the day. This is a NO TOLERANCE rule.
  • Absolutely NO SMOKING in the Dodge or Belle Isle pits. This is a NO TOLERANCE rule. (Jet-A and methanol fuels are stored here!)

1) In consideration of the viewing rights of others, all photographers are requested to photograph while sitting in your seat.

2) Thunderfest reserves the right to deny access or to remove any person without proper tickets or credentials and any person who is disruptive.

3) TICKETS ARE NOT REFUNDABLE. In case postponement of the race is necessary, tickets will be honored on the next scheduled race day. Save your ticket stub!!
