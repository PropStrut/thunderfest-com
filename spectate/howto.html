How To Order Tickets

All ticket plans are available through the Spirit of Detroit Thunderfest Office and a printed ticket brochure is available.

Order methods: Toll-free (800) 359-7760 * Mail * Walk-in * Fax
For mail and fax orders, print the ORDER FORM.

Pay by: Check, money order, Visa, MasterCard or Discover.
For tickets delivered by mail, add $5 (per order) for Shipping and Handling.

For General Admission Parks #4, #7, #8 and #9, tickets are also available from Ticketmaster through early July. (248) 645-6666 - surcharges may apply.
