The Chrysler Jeep Detroit APBA Gold Cup

FREE Hydro Friday!

Friday, July 10

Head out of work early or take an extra long Friday lunch to drive down to the Detroit River and watch the mighty hydroplanes qualify for absolutely FREE!

After the Unlimiteds qualify, Friday's second feature is the "Automotive Gold Cup" race, a match race between the automotive powered Unlimited Lights and the Grand Prix hydroplanes. This race runs at about 5pm.

FREE day allows spectators to view the action from unreserved grandstand seats in Waterworks Park and allows fans to roam through the Hydromart area and enjoy simulators, interactive displays, concessions, and much more. Pit access and parking are not included.

See the parking information for directions to Waterworks Park.

See you there!
