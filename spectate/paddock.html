Dodge Pits - Paddock Area

This is the Dodge Pits paddock area. The area is a walkway between the boats and the team trailers, so you can get a closeup look at both.

On the weekend, without a reserved seat in an adjacent area you'll be hard pressed to watch much racing from here, because space is at a premium in the pits.
