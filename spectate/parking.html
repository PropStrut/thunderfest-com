The Chrysler Jeep Detroit APBA Gold Cup
Parking and Shuttle Information

NOTICE! Due to long-term City of Detroit construction in Waterworks Park, parking is limited and exact details change year to year.

General Admission areas #7 (Memorial Annex), #8 (Henderson) and #9 (Owen) have on-site parking at a nominal fee.

General Admission areas #3 (Waterworks Singles/Groups) and #4 (Waterworks Annex) parking is in Henderson Park for a nominal fee. A free shuttle is provided.

Shuttle service from Henderson Park to all other spectator parks runs from 6 a.m. to 8 p.m.

For all reserved seating areas, you should receive parking instructions with your order.

RV Information

Overnight camper & R/V parking is available on Belle Isle. This area is operated by the Belle Isle division of the Detroit Parks & Recreation Department. Parking is available Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights of raceweek. Rates for 2001 (tentative for 2002) were $35 for one night, $60 for two nights and $90 for all three. Registration is on-site, and the Belle Isle Parks & Rec starts taking reservations Thursday morning of raceweek. For more information contact the Detroit Recreation Department at 313-852-4075 and see our Belle Isle Map page for directions.
